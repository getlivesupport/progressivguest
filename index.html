What if your payment is late?

Late bills can create stress, but understanding the grace period helps you act faster.

1

Due date arrives

If your payment is late, check whether your policy is still in the grace period before assuming the coverage is ended.

2

Grace period runs out

Missed billing may trigger penalties or a cancellation process. The sooner you resolve it, the lower the risk.

3

Reinstatement may be needed

Late balances can require immediate action to restore active coverage, especially near renewal or after a lapse.

Important: If your renewal is approaching, act before the deadline because the grace period may not apply the same way at renewal.
